Before we arrive
  • Shut the main valve (usually near the meter or street side).
  • Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the line.
  • Kill power to any outlet or appliance the water can reach.
  • Move furniture and electronics off wet flooring.

FAQ

Do you charge extra to work evenings or weekends?

Scheduled Mon–Fri work is billed at the standard rate at any hour up to 18:00. The $210 first-hour emergency rate applies to nights, weekends and holidays.

How fast is emergency response?

Target arrival is 90 minutes inside the I-205 loop and same-day in the outer metro. Dispatch gives an honest ETA when you call rather than a promise that is not kept.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes: Oregon CCB licensed, bonded, $2M general liability, and all plumbers hold valid state licences.

Do you handle permits?

Yes, for water heaters, gas lines and repipes. Any permit and inspection cost appears as a separate line on the quote.

Will you tell me when repairing is the wrong call?

Yes. If a line or heater is past economical repair, you get the repair price and the replacement price side by side, with no pressure to choose.

What if the problem comes back?

Labour is covered for two years. Call within that period and the return visit is on us.
